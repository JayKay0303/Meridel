The studio

One studio. One point of contact.

Méridel is an independent web studio based in Geneva. You always talk to the same person: the one who designs, builds and launches your website.

Why “Méridel”

A name, a line.

A meridian is a line that joins two points by the most direct path. That is the idea behind Méridel: connecting your business with the people looking for it, without detours.

It is also a way of working: not too much, not too little. This line runs through our logo, and it follows you on every page of this website.

Approach

A website is your first handshake.

  1. 01

    Clarity

  2. 02

Method

Four steps. Nothing hidden.
