    Questions

    Straight answers.

    What do I need to prepare, and how long does it take?

    Your texts, your photos and your logo, if you have one. The exact timeline is confirmed in the quote, and copywriting can also be handed to us as an option.

    How do feedback and payment work?

    Each plan includes a set number of grouped feedback rounds on the agreed scope. Additions are quoted separately. 50 % deposit at the start, balance after final approval and before launch.

    Who owns the website and the domain?

    The domain is registered in your name and billed separately, like hosting. Once paid in full, you receive the rights, deliverables and access agreed in the quote. Third-party resources remain subject to their licences. Portability terms are set out before the project starts.

    Is maintenance required?

    No. You can add it at launch, or later, whenever you feel the need.

    I already have a website. Can you redesign it?

    Yes. We start from what exists, keep what works and rebuild the rest.

    Can the website be in several languages?

    Yes. One language is included, and others can be added on quote.

    Does a plan speak to you?

    Tell us which one, and we will prepare a quote that fits you.
