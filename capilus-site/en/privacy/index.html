Data protection

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

We respect your privacy. This website is deliberately simple: no accounts, no cookies, no advertising or tracking tools. In line with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), this page explains which data is processed and why.

Data controller

Capilus Coiffure

Avenue de Champel 29, 1206 Geneva, Switzerland

Phone and WhatsApp: +41 22 346 12 60

For any question about your data, contact us by phone, by WhatsApp or by post at the salon’s address.

Visiting the website

When you visit the website, our host’s server (Infomaniak, in Switzerland) automatically records technical information: IP address, date and time, page visited, browser and device type. This information is used only for security, to keep the website running properly and to produce anonymous visitor statistics. It is not used to identify you and is deleted after a limited period.

Appointment requests via WhatsApp

The appointment form does not send or store anything on this website. It only prepares a message (chosen service, first name, availability, any note you add) that opens in WhatsApp. You decide whether to send it.

If you send it, your message, phone number and WhatsApp name are passed to the salon via WhatsApp, a service of Meta Platforms. WhatsApp processes this data under its own privacy policy, including outside Switzerland. The salon uses this information only to answer your request and manage your appointment, and does not share it with anyone.

The same applies if you call or message us directly.

Prices and opening hours

The prices and opening hours shown may be updated from a Google spreadsheet. To load them, your browser contacts Google’s servers, which receive your IP address. No other data is sent. Your browser keeps a copy of these prices and hours (local storage) to display the website faster; it contains no personal data.

External links

The “Directions”, “Google reviews” and “WhatsApp” buttons open services run by Google or Meta. As soon as you use them, their own terms and privacy policies apply.

Fonts and content

Fonts and images are hosted on our own server: no data is sent to a third-⁠party service to display them.

Your rights

You may at any time ask which data about you we process, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its processing. Simply contact us (see above). You may also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), edoeb.admin.ch.

Changes

We may update this policy, for example if the website changes. The version published on this page applies. In case of doubt, the French version prevails.
