Our speciality

Tannin straightening

Capilus Coiffure offers tannin straightening / enzyme therapy by Belmakosmetik. Free from formaldehyde and harsh derived agents.

An organic straightening

An organic straightening with bio-⁠enzymes and natural amino acids. This type of straightening uses an acidic plant protein, mainly obtained from macerated grape seeds and skins (tannin).

The straightening offered by Capilus is for anyone who dreams of smoother hair without a “poker-⁠straight” look, and also for those who want to keep their curls while relaxing, strengthening and caring for them.

No adverse effects for pregnant or breastfeeding women.

Frequently asked questions

Should I avoid straightening if my hair is damaged?

No, quite the opposite! We do it to repair your hair in depth: it is an opportunity to strengthen the hair fibre, which suffers every day (pollution, chemicals…). Technique makes all the difference: this straightening suits every hair type, fine, thick, curly, frizzy and even straight. For straight hair, it simply provides deep nourishment.

How long will my straightening last?

It lasts 4 to 6 months, provided you look after it with paraben-⁠free and sulphate-⁠free shampoos and treatments. Sun, sea and swimming pools can shorten it, so always rinse your hair with clear water after swimming in salt or chlorinated water. Straightening is fully compatible with colouring (except henna) or highlights, although it is best to have them done after the straightening.

How much does straightening cost?

From CHF 200 to 450, depending on the length and thickness of the hair. If you would like an exact quote before booking, I will gladly see you at the salon to discuss it. Allow between 2 and 3½ hours, depending on the length and thickness of the hair.
